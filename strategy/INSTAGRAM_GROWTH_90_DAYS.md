## Strategic proposition / Позиционирование

**North star:** «Мы создаём не каталог красивой одежды, а авторский взгляд на мир моды».

Helena Silk is an author's **fashion-education + visual-inspiration journal**, not a catalogue, verified runway photo agency, designer-label store or professional styling service. It helps people understand *why* an outfit works: silhouette, proportions, design construction, colour harmony, material appearance, accessories and references.

**One-line promise (DE):** „Mode nicht nur anschauen – verstehen, was einen Look besonders macht.“  
**One-line promise (RU):** «Не просто смотреть на моду — замечать, почему образ работает».

Real attraction mechanism: **strong full-length image → one clear insight → useful/saveable explanation → a genuine question → repeated recognizable editorial experience.**

### Two distinct content provenance tracks

- **Track A — Original AI visual editorial:** synthetic fashion illustration, original creative styling analysis. Plain disclosure in bio and every relevant post: *KI-generierte Fashion-Illustration / AI-визуализация, не фотография реального показа*. Never attribute a pictured synthetic garment to Dior/Prada/etc. Do not claim fibre composition from image alone.
- **Track B — Real runway research:** names, dates, historic facts and design techniques grounded in original sources. Authentic third-party photos only if permissions/licences support Instagram usage. Can explain a sourced trend without showing another creator's copyrighted photograph.

Keep source/provenance records for each visual and factual claim. Originality is **not** equivalent to automatically guaranteed reach.

## Target audiences — hypotheses, not measured demographics

**DE profile:** German-speaking people in Germany/Austria/Switzerland who enjoy elegant contemporary fashion, colour harmony, creative silhouettes, and learning how fashion works. Native German voice and German search terms.

**RU profile:** Russian-speaking fashion-curious audience in Germany and across Europe, as well as other places where Instagram is available. Not mechanically translated from German. Local examples, native search terms and comments.

**Promising initial interests:** couture, runway-inspired fashion, visual art/design, accessory and garment-detail observation, style-as-culture. Age, gender mix, income and geographic ranking must be measured; no unsupported demographic targeting.

## Editorial mix — launch hypothesis

| Pillar | Share | What audience gains | Example hook |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Why this look works** | 35% | Saveable explanation of silhouette, colour and proportions | «Почему этот сложный крой кажется гармоничным?» |
| **Colour laboratory** | 20% | Understand useful combinations and contrast | «Фуксия + оранжевый: что удерживает баланс?» |
| **Materials and design details** | 20% | Know what to look for in stitching/texture/drape | «Как складка меняет силуэт?» |
| **Runway research / fashion culture** | 15% | Verified context and designer ideas | «Откуда появилась эта форма жакета?» |
| **Helena's eye / audience conversation** | 10% | Recognizable author, personal taste, genuine discussion | «Что вы заметили первым — цвет или конструкцию?» |

Do not rely on names of luxury houses as the only click magnet. Never invent provenance.

## Publishing formats — small, realistic cadence

**Initial pilot recommendation, PER language profile:** three feed posts per week: **two carousels + one Reel**, plus 2–3 light Stories with polls or behind-the-scenes. That is six localized feed posts total per week across two accounts, based on **three shared editorial concepts**. Reduce cadence if Helena cannot sustain quality.

- **Carousel (3–5 cards):** (1) full-length original look; (2) a close-up/crop of the *same owned/allowed* image; (3) palette/construction diagram or comparison; (4) a practical educational explanation; optionally (5) a natural question. A carousel may contain multiple cards, but each downloadable original image must remain an individual file.
- **Reel (10–25 seconds initially, test rather than assume):** one clear first-second question, controlled motion over original image(s), close-up of a detail, concise voiceover or captions in native language, one final takeaway. Use appropriately licensed sound. No fake designer footage or unlicensed runway recording.
- **Stories:** simple true opinion/poll/quiz: choose colour, notice a detail, ask about a silhouette, reply with an educational answer. Do not spam polls or use manipulative comment bait.
- **Caption:** intriguing observation → specific design explanation → small transferable insight → natural question. Up to **five relevant hashtags** (current Instagram limit), not repeated broad tag dumps.

**Example RU hook:** «Почему фуксия и оранжевый не спорят в одном костюме? Всё дело в линии жакета и спокойных аксессуарах».  
**Example DE hook:** „Warum wirken Fuchsia und Orange in einem Look so stimmig? Die klare Blazerlinie macht den Unterschied.“

## Audience acquisition: ethical discovery loops

1. **Native search and topic clarity:** identifiable subject keywords within name, headline, caption and alt text; 3–5 precise hashtags, e.g. #Farbkombination / #Modedetails / #FashionIllustration / #HelenaSilk; rotate by story.
2. **Reels reach discovery:** test original short video interpretations of still assets; Trial Reels with non-followers if the account is eligible. Measure actual watch time, shares, profile visits.
3. **Save/share-worthy education:** explain a principle people can use later, not only «look how beautiful». Offer comparison of two plausible choices and when they work.
4. **Human community participation:** daily meaningful comments/conversations on *relevant* fashion education, art, design and emerging-creator accounts in each language, with no scripted repetitive comments, follow/unfollow or spam DMs.
5. **Collaborations after a base exists:** 1–2 mutually relevant outreach messages per week to small creators, textile/craft educators, independent European style pages, local boutiques (only if appropriate). Propose a genuine design breakdown or joint Q&A, not link exchange for fake engagement. Use collaboration post tools when applicable.
6. **Own channels:** genuine launch announcements to existing personal networks only when appropriate and not coerced; invite feedback on best concepts, share profiles via link.
7. **Optional paid distribution:** postpone until at least 4–6 weeks of organic signals. If testing, use modest, capped budget and one proven Reel/post per audience, with clear objective and verified eligibility. Do not promise results.

### Do NOT do

Buy followers or likes, automate mass comments, use engagement bots, pretend to be a real fashion press photographer, use unauthorized runway images or misleading designer labels, publish fifty nearly identical photos in one burst, copy-paste the same DE/RU literal caption, chase fake hashtags or guaranteed reach claims.

## Measurement: weekly dashboard per account and content format

Collect at least: posts published; reach (especially non-followers); views; total watch time / average watch time when shown; shares/sends; saves; meaningful comments; profile visits; follows attributable if available; new followers; bio-link taps; and posting time.

**Useful derived indicators:**
- Save rate = saves / accounts reached
- Share rate = shares or sends / accounts reached
- Profile visit rate = profile visits / accounts reached
- Follow conversion = new follows / profile visits
- Reel average watch time = watch time / plays (if source data supports it)

**Do not use absolute thresholds invented before data exists.** Weeks 1–4 establish baseline. Compare similar types over 4-week periods, DE and RU separately, and note when an outside collaborator drives an outlier.

## Resource economics

Do **not** assume 40 images imply 40 ready posts. Several batches contain alternate designs, and all AI images are drafts requiring owner selection and accurate labels. A sustainable pipeline is:

`Approved idea → image provenance/rights → selected artwork → editorial analysis → DE text + RU text → format production → human QA → schedule → weekly analytics → lesson logged`.

Start with 6–9 best diverse assets, not all 40. Reuse ideas for different editorial formats, not repetitive identical posts. Keep one shared `content_id` mapping the two language executions and source asset.

**Recommended ownership:** Helena = creative/factual/editorial approval; ChatGPT = research, draft strategy, localized copy and visual concepts; Claude Code/Codex = repository ingestion/asset automation and optionally analytics report scripts, **not** autonomous publication without explicit owner approval.

## First 10 actions (suggested order)

1. Helena selects the best **9** non-repetitive visuals (and rejects duplicates).
2. Confirm profile handles and the two-language account model.
3. Create bios, transparent AI disclosure, highlights and 3 pinned introductory posts.
4. Create 9 paired DE/RU captions with one useful editorial insight each.
5. Turn 3 of those stories into 15–20s Reels; keep real full-look image first.
6. Establish a simple sheet or GitHub CSV with post ID, asset SHA, DE/RU, format, dates and measured results.
7. Launch profiles softly and invite genuine feedback from relevant people.
8. For two weeks publish at a maintainable cadence, answer comments and test polls.
9. Evaluate reach → visits → follows plus save/share rates, not just likes.
10. At day 30 choose two winning themes; only then consider small collaborations or ads.
