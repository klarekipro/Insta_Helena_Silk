## The carousel template

1. Full-height original fashion look; no false brand labels.
2. Crop/close-up of the owned visual showing the first design principle.
3. Simple visual contrast or color swatches and clearly labeled interpretation.
4. One useful rule for readers; one real question.

**Reel template** (start with 10–25 seconds, measure): first second question → image slow pan → one important detail → short explanation in German or Russian voiceover/subtitles → close with a natural thought. Reel and static post files may be separately exported; never overwrite original PNG.

## Activities beyond posts

- 2–3 Stories per profile per week, using polls with authentic questions and replies.
- 15–20 minutes on posting days for thoughtful real interaction in relevant fashion/art conversations. No forced engagement quotas and no generic repetitive comments.
- Record weekly metrics per language: nonfollower reach, saves/reach, sends/reach, profile visits/reach, follows/visits, Reel watch time.
- Weekly 20-minute editorial review with Helena: one strongest post, one weakest, one learning and next test.

## Launch communications

**No mass DM, no fake giveaways, no bought followers.** Helena may share an authentic invitation with acquaintances who genuinely appreciate fashion. Future micro-collabs should be relevant and fair, not swaps for fake engagement.

## Day 30 decision

- Which two concepts received the best save/share and qualified follower response?
- Which format contributes non-follower discovery and which retains community?
- Is the DE/RU workload sustainable?
- What should Batch 05 imagery support (new editorial gaps rather than more of the same)?

Do not start paid advertisements until there are meaningful organic comparison signals and an owner-approved spending limit.
