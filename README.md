## Our north star

> **Мы создаём не каталог красивой одежды, а авторский взгляд на мир моды.**
>
> *Wir zeigen nicht einfach schöne Mode. Wir zeigen Helenas persönlichen Blick darauf.*

Helena's editorial signature is thoughtful, educational analysis guided by her own eye: a surprising colour pairing, the movement of a fabric, an unusual seam, a bag that changes the story of an outfit. Every publication gives the audience a reason to **notice, understand, feel and return**.

A generic caption says «Тренды сезона: красный цвет». Helena Silk asks a sharper educational question: «Почему красный и шоколадный могут работать в одном образе? Разберём пропорции и материалы».

## Mission

Create an independent, aesthetically coherent fashion community based on genuine curiosity and a distinctive personal voice. Inspire, inform and grow a trusting audience; do not invent professional credentials or assume that services or products are already offered.

## Publishing model

**Working hypothesis:** two Instagram accounts, one German and one Russian. They share one editorial source, visual identity and factual research, but each language needs **native adaptation**, not mechanical translation. Hooks, examples, questions, keywords and posting times may differ. Evaluate accounts separately.

## Editorial scope

Paris, Milan and Spanish fashion events; designers and collections; silhouettes, accessories, fabric textures, craftsmanship, colours, hidden details, cultural stories and wearable inspiration. Always distinguish the date of a show from the season it presents.

## Principles

1. Personal observation before generic trend lists.
2. Every image supports an editorial story, not the reverse.
3. Distinguish opinion from sourced facts.
4. Never misrepresent an AI illustration as an authentic fashion-week photo.
5. Check image licences; credit alone is not usage permission.
6. Use useful insights and authentic conversations, not engagement bait or spam.
7. Measure genuine audience interest: meaningful comments, shares, saves and profile visits.

## Three questions before every publication

- Why would someone stop scrolling?
- What would they notice, learn or feel?
- Why would they return to **Helena's** perspective?
