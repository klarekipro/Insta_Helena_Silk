## QA and limitations

- Ten separate images, not ten panels of one collage.
- 1024 × 1536 pixel portrait originals; no forced crop of footwear or garment hems.
- Ten distinct SHA-256 checksums. A 64-bit difference-hash screening against Batch 01–03 found nearest distances of 15–25 bits (non-identical images), but this does not prove complete semantic novelty.
- There is **visual repetition within Batch 04**: multiple blue, yellow, silver and green outfits, including similarly sculptural silhouettes. These are drafts for the owner's selection, not ten distinct approved concepts.
- This batch intentionally experiments with stronger color contrasts than earlier batches, particularly fuchsia/orange tailoring.
- Bilingual captions in [BATCH_04_CAPTIONS_DE_RU.md](BATCH_04_CAPTIONS_DE_RU.md) are drafts and refer only to visible stylistic properties.
- No fashion house attribution, verified fabric composition or documentary claim is established by the AI visuals.
- No Instagram post has been published or approved.
