# Research, Attribution and Image Rights

## Source hierarchy

Prefer fashion house and official Fashion Week sources for runway facts and collection attribution. Use established fashion publications for independent reporting. Keep specific URLs with each claim and distinguish confirmed details from editorial interpretation.

## Image permissions

- **Licensed photography:** retain creator, licence holder, permitted platforms, duration, territory, modifications, credit requirements and proof.
- **Official press imagery:** do not assume public availability means free repost rights; inspect terms or get permission.
- **Original creator photography:** record ownership and any releases needed.
- **AI visuals:** clearly label as illustration / AI-generated imagery and never claim they document a real show or are an exclusive photograph.

**Attribution is not equivalent to permission.** Do not upload third-party copyrighted runway images into this repository without proper rights.

## Fact-checking

Record country, event, runway date, designer/house, season, collection, referenced look and exact factual sources. A Fall/Winter 2026–2027 look may have been shown in 2026, while Spring/Summer 2027 may also have been shown in 2026. Do not assume availability of future images or shows.

## Instagram ethics

No fake engagement, purchased followers, bot interactions or algorithm gaming. Use genuine descriptive SEO terms, accessible alt text and a small number of topic-specific hashtags. No claims that a posting tactic guarantees reach.
